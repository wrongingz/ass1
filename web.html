Plantation Activities

Pruning
Carefully trimming older branches to improve sunlight penetration, airflow, and future coffee bean yields.
Harvesting
Hand-picking ripe, red coffee cherries at the peak of maturity to ensure premium flavor quality.
Drying
Spread out and drying freshly processed coffee beans under the sun to reduce moisture to optimal levels.
